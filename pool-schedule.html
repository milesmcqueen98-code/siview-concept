Pool rules

  • Shower before you enter the pool.
  • To use the deep water, pass a swim test: swim the width of the pool on your front with your face in the water, then on your back.
  • Children under 6 need a parent in the water, within arm's reach.
  • Children under 3, and anyone not potty trained, wear a swim diaper.

How this page answers the RFP

Numbers match the markers on the page. Q numbers refer to the District's answers to proposers' questions.

  1. 1Opens on today

    The schedule opens on the current day; arrow keys move between days. Staff edit one schedule each season (Q27), and the homepage shows today's times from it.

  2. 2Closures first

    Holiday closures sit above the schedule, and the homepage alert links here.

  3. 3Fees as real tables

    Drop-in fees and passes are separate tables with captions and header cells (WCAG 1.3.1). Buying a pass opens Dash.
