Swim lessons

Learn-to-swim classes for infants through adults at Si View Pool, taught by instructors certified in the American Red Cross curriculum.

Find your level

Current students: your online progress report shows which level to choose next. New swimmers can request a swim evaluation.

Little ones learn with a parent in the water, building comfort and early water-safety habits.

Infant and toddler skills checklist (PDF)

Small group classes that build basic swimming skills and confidence for young swimmers.
