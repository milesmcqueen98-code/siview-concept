Swim lessons

Si View Pool is an American Red Cross Learn-to-Swim facility. Group lessons run from ages 6 months through adult, and private lessons from age 3.

Register for group lessons Register for private lessons

Choose a level

Pick the level that matches the cautious end of the swimmer's skills. Most swimmers need about 10 hours of lessons to finish a level, which is longer than one session.

Current students can check their online progress report. Not sure where to start? Book a free 10-minute swim assessment with Josiah Stoddard at jstoddard@siviewpark.org.

  • Infant and toddler

    6 months to 3½ years

    Parent Tot (6 months to 3 years) and Puddle Jumpers I and II (2½ to 3½). A parent is in the water for Parent Tot and Puddle Jumpers I.

    Infant and toddler skills (PDF)

  • Preschool

    3 to 6 years

    Preschool 1 to 5. Children must turn 3 before the session ends.

    Preschool skills (PDF)

  • Youth

    6 to 12 years

    Youth 1 to 6. Children who are 5½ can join with preschool skills. Youth 5 and 6 are 45-minute lessons.

    Youth skills (PDF)

  • Intro to SwimNew

    5½ to 12 years

    A 30-minute small class for children who are hesitant or fearful around water. It builds comfort and readiness for Youth 1.

  • Adult

    Adults

    Beginner, intermediate and advanced. Beginners should be comfortable in shallow water; deep water is not required.

    Adult levels and skills (PDF)

  • Private lessons

    3 and older

    For students who do better one-on-one. Openings are limited. Two students can share a lesson if their skills are close.

The developmental swim team prepares swimmers for USA Swimming teams. Finishing Youth 6 first is recommended, and the team follows the same registration dates as lessons.

Fees

Sessions run 3 to 8 lessons. You pay the per-lesson price times the number of lessons in your session.

Example: an 8-lesson Preschool session costs $112 for residents. Private lessons are booked for the full session. Need-based scholarships are available all year; contact the pool before you register, and allow about a week for approval.

How to register

  1. New to Si View? Create an account in Dash, the District's registration system, and make sure your street address is current.
  2. Register when your session opens. Register for no more than one class per week.
  3. Class full? Join the waitlist. Staff contact waitlisted families when a space opens.

You can also register by phone at 425-831-1900, or in person at the pool or the administrative office.

Check the District boundary map (PDF), or update your address at 425-414-0765 or poolstaff@siviewpark.org.

Before your first lesson

  • Visit during Public Swim beforehand so your child can get to know the pool, locker rooms and deck.
  • Arrive no more than 5 minutes before class, dressed in swimwear, with a towel.
  • Children who are not fully potty trained wear a swim diaper with snug legs.
  • Save snacks for after the lesson.
  • Parents watch from the bleachers. Know your child's instructor and level, such as Pre 1 or Youth 1.

Thinking about goggles? Read the goggles information sheet (PDF).

Policies

There are no makeup classes for missed lessons. Students are welcome at the remaining lessons in their session.

If the District cancels a class for weather or pool contamination, you receive a credit for that class.

Refunds for courses in progress follow the District's registration policy. Students cannot transfer between classes mid-session.

Requests are welcome, but they depend on staff schedules and cannot be guaranteed.

Staff may withdraw a student from a level that is beyond their ability, so choose the level that matches the cautious end of their skills.

How this page answers the RFP

Numbers match the markers on the page. Q numbers refer to the District's answers to proposers' questions.

  1. 1Straight to Dash

    Both buttons open Dash filtered to group or private lessons, using the District's current links (Q5).

  2. 2Dates entered once

    The next registration date shows here and on the homepage from one entry, and comes down after it passes.

  3. 3Levels in plain text

    Ages and rules are text that staff can edit and screen readers can read. Each program links its skills checklist.

  4. 4Tables with header cells

    Each price is read with its row and column (WCAG 1.3.1). Today's fee tables have no header cells.

  5. 5Links that say where they go

    Each PDF link names the document. Today four links on this page read only “Click” (WCAG 2.4.4).

  6. 6Short page, full detail

    Policies sit in sections that open by keyboard and announce whether they are open.
