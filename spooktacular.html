The Great Si View Spooktacular

A haunted house, Halloween games, pumpkin decorating and a costume contest, for preschool and elementary kids and their families. Costumes encouraged.

Date
Saturday, October 24, 2026
Sessions
Session 1: noon to 1:15 p.m.
Session 2: 1:30 to 2:45 p.m.
Place
Si View Community Center, 400 SE Orchard Drive, North Bend
Tickets
Ages 2 and up: $18 for residents, $20 for non-residents. Under 2: free, but get a ticket so staff can plan space. Adults do not need tickets.
Get tickets in Dash

Add to your calendar: Session 1 Session 2

How to get tickets

  1. Open Drop-In Events in your Dash account, or use the Get tickets button above.
  2. Choose October 24, then pick a session and a ticket type.
  3. Buy a ticket for each child. You do not need to register each child by name.

How this page answers the RFP

Numbers match the markers on the page. Q numbers refer to the District's answers to proposers' questions.

  1. 1The event template

    Date, time, place, cost and tickets sit in the same place on every event page. The price comes from the event record, so it matches Dash.

  2. 2Tickets in Dash

    Opens the Dash drop-in events list for October 24, where tickets are sold today.

  3. 3Add to calendar

    Each session downloads as a calendar file that opens on phones and in Outlook.
